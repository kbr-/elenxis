# Library loans

## Borrowing

A member may borrow a copy that is not on loan.

A copy on loan is never lent to a second member.

A member may have at most five copies on loan at once.

A member who owes 10 or more in fines may not borrow.

A copy that another member holds may not be borrowed.

When a member borrows a copy they hold, their hold on it ends.

The loan period depends on the membership:

| Membership | Loan period |
| --- | --- |
| standard | 14 days |
| premium | 28 days |

A loan is due one loan period after the day it starts.

## Renewing

A loan may be renewed. It may be renewed at most twice, and never while another member holds the
copy.

Renewing a loan moves its due date one loan period past the old due date.

A loan cannot be renewed after its due date.

## Returning

Returning a copy ends its loan.

A copy returned after its due date adds a fine of 1 for each day late.

A copy returned on or before its due date adds no fine.

Returning a copy that is not on loan changes nothing.

## Holds

A member may hold a copy that is on loan to another member.

A member may not hold a copy they have on loan.

A copy has at most one hold.

## Fines

Paying reduces a member's fines by the amount paid, and never below zero.

## Refusals

A request that is refused changes nothing.
